Material You style coffee app that tracks your current supply of coffee beans, provides logs to help you dial in , and more. This is written for my own personal use and I hope some of you may find it handy, however I cannot promise quick bug fixes/feature additions

Working Features

- Ability to add the name of the coffee, the roaster name, tasting notes, weight and roasted on date
- Reset button to reset app back to factory settings
- Ability to tick if you have ordered a new bag or not - a value which is reset when you edit the bag name or roaster
- Small green, yellow or red dot depending on time since roast date
- Brew ratio calculator, allowing you to quickly see required coffee and water if you use a different amount to what you're used to (along with water amounts broken down to my personal recipe
- Temporarily change brew ratio calculated
- Preset buttons that remove specific amounts of coffee from the total
- Input your own personal ratios, add your own dose presets, and add brew method and grinder pairings to help keep track of dialling in
- Handy dialling in logs, which do not appear on the home screen unless you specifically begin tracking - you can enter your grind size, dose in and water weight in/weight out, time, what you taste and how you want to change it next time
- Once you are happy with your result, press the green Dialled In button, and not only are all the logs for that brew method removed, your grind size, weight in and out and time are kept on your home screen in case you forget
- cleaning section to keep track of maintenance for your equipment
